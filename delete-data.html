Delete your data

Honeycomb War has no server and no accounts of its own. The developer never receives or stores your game data, so there is nothing to ask the developer to delete. You can delete everything yourself, at any time:

Progress saved in Google Drive

Signing in with Google is optional. If you signed in, your progress is one encrypted file, “honeycombwar.save”, in a folder called “Honeycomb War” in your own Google Drive.

  1. Open drive.google.com and delete the “Honeycomb War” folder, then empty it from the Bin.
  2. Remove the game's access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Progress saved on your device

  • Android app: uninstall the app, or go to Settings › Apps › Honeycomb War › Storage › Clear storage.
  • Website: clear this site's data (cookies and site data) in your browser's settings.

Ads

The Android app's optional “Support the author” button shows an ad from Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising ID. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings › Google › Ads (or Settings › Privacy › Ads) on your phone. Google's data practices are described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

See also the Honeycomb War privacy policy.
