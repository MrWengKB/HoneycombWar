Privacy

Honeycomb War has no server of its own. Your game data is never sent to the developer.

Without signing in, your level, record and the game in progress are saved only in this browser's local storage, encrypted.

If you sign in with Google, the game asks for your name and email address (to show who is signed in) and for Google Drive access limited to files the game creates itself (the drive.file permission). Your progress is saved as one encrypted file, “honeycombwar.save”, in a folder called “Honeycomb War” in your own Google Drive. The game cannot see any other files in your Drive, and nothing is sent to the developer.

Website statistics. The website uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are used. It uses cookies and collects information such as your approximate location, device and browser type and the pages you open. It never includes your game data, level, the names you enter or your Google Drive files. The Android app does not use Google Analytics. You can block it with your browser's privacy settings or a content blocker, and the game still works.

In the Android app, progress is saved the same way: in the app's own storage on your phone, and in your Google Drive if you sign in. The optional “Support the author” button shows one rewarded ad from Google AdMob, which may collect device information such as the advertising ID to show and measure the ad; see how Google uses this information. No ad is shown unless you tap that button.

You can remove the game's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and delete your data by deleting the “Honeycomb War” folder in Google Drive and clearing this site's data in your browser (or uninstalling the app). Step by step: Delete your data.

See also the general MrWengKB's Creations privacy policy.

隐私

蜂窝战争没有自己的服务器，你的游戏数据不会传给开发者。

不登录时，你的等级、战绩和进行中的对局只会加密保存在这个浏览器的本地存储里。

如果你使用 Google 登录，游戏会请求你的姓名和电子邮件地址（用来显示谁已登录），以及仅限于游戏自己创建的文件的 Google 云端硬盘访问权限（drive.file 权限）。你的进度会保存为一个加密文件“honeycombwar.save”，放在你自己的 Google 云端硬盘中名为“Honeycomb War”的文件夹里。游戏看不到你云端硬盘里的其他任何文件，也不会向开发者发送任何内容。

网站统计。网站使用 Google Analytics 统计访问次数和页面使用情况。它会使用 cookie，并收集大致位置、设备和浏览器类型、打开的页面等信息，但绝不包含你的游戏数据、等级、你输入的名字或 Google 云端硬盘文件。安卓应用不使用 Google Analytics。你可以用浏览器的隐私设置或内容拦截工具阻止它，游戏照样能玩。

在安卓应用中，进度的保存方式相同：保存在手机上应用自己的存储里，如果你登录了，也会保存在你的 Google 云端硬盘里。可选的“支持作者”按钮会显示一则来自 Google AdMob 的激励广告，AdMob 可能会收集广告 ID 等设备信息来展示和衡量广告；请参阅 Google 如何使用这些信息。除非你点按该按钮，否则不会显示任何广告。

你可以随时在 myaccount.google.com/permissions 撤销游戏的访问权限；删除 Google 云端硬盘中的“Honeycomb War”文件夹并在浏览器中清除本网站的数据（或卸载应用），即可删除你的数据。详细步骤：删除你的数据。

另请参阅总体的 MrWengKB's Creations 隐私政策。
