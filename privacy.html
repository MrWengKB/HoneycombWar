Privacy

Honeycomb War has no server of its own and does not collect any data.

Without signing in, your level, record and the game in progress are saved only in this browser's local storage, encrypted.

If you sign in with Google, the game asks for your name and email address (to show who is signed in) and for Google Drive access limited to files the game creates itself (the drive.file permission). Your progress is saved as one encrypted file, “honeycombwar.save”, in a folder called “Honeycomb War” in your own Google Drive. The game cannot see any other files in your Drive, and nothing is sent to the developer.

You can remove the game's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and delete your data by deleting the “Honeycomb War” folder in Google Drive and clearing this site's data in your browser.

See also the general MrWengKB's Creations privacy policy.
